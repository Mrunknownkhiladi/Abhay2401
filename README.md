## 🚀 About Me

I work where code meets hardware. On the software side, I build **Java full stack applications**: backend services with **Spring Boot**, clean **REST APIs**, and **database-driven** systems on MySQL and PostgreSQL, with web front ends on top.

On the engineering side, my **IoT** and **embedded systems** work connects sensors and microcontrollers to the software around them, and my **electrical and communication** background covers electronics, networking, and wireless technologies. My **mechanical engineering** grounding in production, CNC, and manufacturing keeps my designs tied to how things are actually built and used.

What ties it together is **automation** and **real-world engineering solutions**: systems that sense, communicate, process data, and act.
